## 0. What Igor rejected in v0.6 (measured on the live form, 1280 / 1180x820 / 820x1180)

- `.rub-wrap` (overflow auto both axes) overflows by exactly 1 px wide and 3 px tall at every
  viewport (scrollWidth 969 vs clientWidth 968; scrollHeight 830 vs 827), so scrollbars paint
  under and beside the rubric for nothing. He wants them gone.
- The `+` is a 20 px circle with a TEXT "+" glyph, 0.8 px from the chip's bottom edge and 4 px
  from the right: it rides the border and the glyph sits off-centre. He wants it inset, centred.
- The note panel is a table row (`tr.rub-note-row`) that opens under the chip's row; for the last
  rows it lands at the bottom of the table and nobody notices it opened. He wants a POP-UP
  anchored to the chip, obviously open, with a proper animation.
- The badge blue `#1257FF` is too loud; the full-saturation green / yellow / red chips read as a
  Christmas tree. He wants subtle.

## 1. Rubric wrapper: no scrollbars

- `.rub-wrap`: `overflow: visible` by default. Only under `@media (max-width: 759px)` may it be
  `overflow-x: auto` (the table keeps `min-width: 700px` for phones). Never `overflow-y: auto`.
- Also remove the CAUSE of the 1 px / 3 px overflow (sub-pixel table width, border or shadow, the
  absolutely positioned button, whatever it is): with `overflow` temporarily forced to `auto` in a
  test, `scrollWidth === clientWidth` and `scrollHeight === clientHeight` must hold on `.rub-wrap`
  at 1280x900, 1180x820 and 820x1180. `body.scrollWidth === body.clientWidth` stays true.

## 2. The `+` note button

- Same element and attributes as v0.6 (`button.rub-note-btn`, sibling of `.rub-chip` inside
  `.rub-cell`, `data-level`, `data-n`, aria-label, `.has-note`), same click behaviour.
- Geometry: 22 x 22 px circle, `right: 6px; bottom: 6px` inside the chip box (measure: the
  button rect's right edge is 6 px left of the chip rect's right edge, its bottom edge 6 px above
  the chip's bottom edge, at every viewport). Hit area at least 36 x 36 px (a transparent
  `::before` or padding box), the visible circle stays 22 px.
- Glyph: NO text glyph. Two bars drawn with pseudo-elements (each 1.5 px x 10 px, centred with
  `left:50%; top:50%; transform: translate(-50%, -50%)` plus a 90 deg rotation for the second), so
  the plus is pixel-centred in the circle.
- The chip text must never touch the button: reserve space (bottom padding of about 32 px, or
  a bottom-right float spacer, builder's choice). Assert it: for all 32 chips at 1280 and 820,
  no client rect of the chip's text (Range.getClientRects over the text node) intersects the
  button rect.
- Colours: empty = transparent fill, 1 px border `rgba(20, 54, 66, .28)`, bars
  `rgba(20, 54, 66, .55)`; hover/focus-visible border `.45`. Filled (`.has-note`) = solid
  `var(--navy)` (#143642, the form's AIS navy ink), white bars, no other colour. Press feedback
  on pointer-down: `transform: scale(.97)` over 100 ms. Dark theme: same idea on the dark tokens
  (border/bars from the dark ink at the same alphas, filled = `var(--paper)` of dark mode or an
  equivalent solid that reads on the dark chip). Print: filled badge prints as a solid dot.
- Badge pop: when a note becomes non-empty through USER INPUT (typing or a pad extraction), the
  button plays a one-shot 220 ms scale keyframe 1 -> 1.12 -> 1. Not on load / draft restore /
  record load (those set `.has-note` silently).

## 3. The pop-up (`.rub-note-pop`) and scrim (`.rub-note-scrim`)

- ONE persistent pop-up element and ONE persistent scrim, created once at rubric build time
  inside the rubric section (the section gets `position: relative`). Both are ALWAYS mounted:
  state lives on `data-state="open" | "closed"`. Closed = `opacity: 0`, `transform: scale(.94)
  translateY(6px)`, `pointer-events: none`, `aria-hidden="true"`. Never toggle `display`,
  `hidden` or `visibility` on them (iOS Safari raster rule), never `position: fixed` (the v0.58
  keyboard engine must stay untouched; the pop-up is `position: absolute` inside the rubric
  section and scrolls with the page). The scrim is `position: absolute; inset: 0` of the rubric
  section only, `background: rgba(20, 54, 66, .16)`, same open/closed states.
- Contents (the same as the v0.6 row, restyled as a card): header `<Level> <n> · <State>` with a
  small state dot; the criterion text in small type; the textarea (its `id` is set on open to
  `sp1_note_<level>_<n>` of the open criterion, no `name`, 3 rows, grows to 8); pencil
  `button.pad-field-btn` and paperclip `button.pad-attach` with `data-pad-target` set on open to
  `sp1_<level>_<n>_note` (pencil hidden in record view / when the pad is unavailable, paperclip
  hidden unless pages exist for that target); `Done`. Card: white (dark: card token), 1 px edge,
  12 px radius, soft shadow `0 12px 32px rgba(20, 54, 66, .18)`, width 380 px capped at 92% of
  the rubric section width.
- Placement (computed from the chip's `+` button rect, relative to the rubric section): right of
  the chip (`left = chip.right + 8`, `top = chip.top`) when it fits inside the section; else left
  of the chip (`left = chip.left - width - 8`); else below the chip (`top = chip.bottom + 8`,
  left aligned to the chip and clamped). Always clamped inside the section (8 px margin) on both
  axes. After opening, if the card's bottom is below the visual viewport, call
  `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest', behavior: 'smooth' })` on the card (an ordinary scroll, not
  a scroll lock). Reposition on `resize` and `orientationchange` while open. `transform-origin`
  = the `+` button's centre expressed in the card's own coordinates, so the card grows out of
  the button it came from (and shrinks back into it).
- Open: tap `+`. Close: Done, tap on the scrim, Esc, or tapping the same `+`. Tapping another
  chip's `+` while open re-anchors: the card closes toward its button (exit) and materialises at
  the new one; interrupting mid-transition is fine (CSS transitions re-target from the current
  value). Tapping a chip (the colour cycle) never opens or closes the pop-up. Colour changes
  while open update the header live (as v0.6).
- No auto-focus on open (Pencil users must not get the keyboard). Focus moves to the textarea
  only when the observer taps it.
- Motion, register "materialize + settle" (Apple: damping 1.0 / response 0.3, no overshoot;
  the dashboard's GSAP grammar done in CSS because the form has no GSAP):
  open = `transform` 280 ms `cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1)` (scale .94 -> 1, translateY 6px -> 0)
  and `opacity` 200 ms, scrim `opacity` 200 ms; close = the same path back, 200 ms,
  `cubic-bezier(.64, 0, .78, 0)` (mirrored easing, Apple spatial consistency); `+` press
  `scale(.97)` 100 ms on `:active`; badge pop per section 2. Only `transform` and `opacity`
  animate. `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`: opacity-only 150 ms, no scale/translate.
- Record / locked view: the pop-up opens read-only (textarea `readonly`, pencil hidden,
  paperclip shown when pages exist), never writes the draft (hard rule 13).
- Remove the v0.6 `tr.rub-note-row` row and its CSS entirely; `syncRubricNotes`,
  `sp1_notes`, `sp1_selected_text`, badges, draft rules and the pad extraction landing keep
  working exactly as v0.6 (the extraction lands in the open criterion's textarea when its
  pop-up is open, and in the hidden data regardless).

## 4. Calm palette (chips, legend, record view, print)

- Tokens on `:root` (light) with dark-theme overrides under the existing dark selector:
  `--rub-present-bg: #E8F3EC; --rub-present-edge: #2F7D4F; --rub-partial-bg: #FBF3DC;
  --rub-partial-edge: #C28E0E; --rub-absent-bg: #FBE9EA; --rub-absent-edge: #B23B3B;`
  Dark: same hues as translucent tints over the dark chip (e.g. `rgba(47,125,79,.24)`,
  `rgba(194,142,14,.22)`, `rgba(178,59,59,.24)`) with the edges unchanged.
- Chip states: `background: var(--rub-<state>-bg)`, `border-left: 3px solid var(--rub-<state>-edge)`,
  text stays the normal chip ink (NOT white), the other three borders keep the chip's usual
  edge colour. Hover keeps the same tint (no darkening jump). The untouched (white) chip is
  unchanged.
- Legend swatches (`.rub-legend-sw[data-state]`): the same tint with the same 3 px left edge
  (a 12-14 px square); the empty swatch unchanged. Legend and hint text unchanged (v0.6 wording).
- Print: tints and edges print as-is (`print-color-adjust: exact` where the v0.6 print rules
  forced colours). Record view identical to the form.
- Footer `Form · Progress in Lessons OTP · otp-v0.7`. `<title>` unchanged.

criterion and typing produces the v0.6 `sp1_notes` / `sp1_selected_text` values unchanged;

edges, text colour not white; the legend swatches match; (g) the filled badge resolves to
`#143642`; (h) reduced-motion media query present (a CSS text assertion is enough); (i) payload
still exactly the 31 keys; (j) zero console errors, body has no horizontal scroll at the three
