How these are counted. Average progress is the best rating recorded for each teacher, then averaged across teachers. Each numbered marker opens the supporting evidence; identifying teacher details are withheld.

In summary

Average progress across the round is Good. About half of the lessons observed are Good or better, about a third are Acceptable, and about one in ten sit below that.

  • Most consistent strength. Questioning is used to check understanding rather than to move the lesson along.12
  • What that looks like. The follow up question, not the first question, is what moves the group.13
  • Where it holds. Modelling is explicit and the success criteria stay visible throughout.27
  • Main focus. Where progress stalls, the pace moves on before every group has answered.41
