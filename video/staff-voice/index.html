What the observer receives

Acceptable, and how to reach Good

Step three of that loop is not a paragraph of advice. It is a note built from the teacher's own lesson, showing where they sit now, the band above, and the specific change that closes the gap. The observer approves it, edits it, or writes their own.

Questioning is the lever here. The evidence shows understanding being checked with the students who volunteer, and the lesson moving on before the rest have answered. One change to the questioning routine moves this from Acceptable to Good.

Questioning

Follow every answer with one question that asks the student to justify it, before you move on.

Look for
Students explaining their reasoning without being asked twice, including those who did not volunteer.

Cold call, then "how do you know?". Name the student, pause, then ask the follow-up, so the whole class stays with the thinking rather than waiting for the answer.

What makes it usable

Five strategies, mapped to the rubric

Five coaching strategies with worked examples, sitting against the Student Progress Rubric point by point and built on the AIS Teaching and Learning Agenda. The observer and the analysis draw from the same shelf, so the plan the teacher receives is recognisably ours, and drafting it takes the observer minutes rather than an evening.

Rubric point
·Students know what success looks like before they begin
·Questioning checks understanding rather than moves the lesson on
·Work is adapted so every group is stretched

Who sees what

By role, not one report for everyone

Leadership

Themes across the school rather than a folder of individual replies. Where support is being asked for, which challenges repeat and where, each claim carrying its references.

Middle leaders

Their own team and nobody else's. Not a report to read through, a starting point for a coaching conversation with the teacher's own words already in front of them.

The teacher

A plan of their own, built from their own lesson and their own words, in language the school already uses. The same thing the observation gives them now.
