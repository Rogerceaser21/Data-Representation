How the OTP Progress form works

For AIS Leadership · Igor Sesar · 14 September 2026

Observe, agree next steps, and start the next lap from them. Colours, not scores.

One coaching lap, and what the form does

Live nowComing

Before the first lap: each teacher self-evaluates against the OTP (a Google Form today; the Teacher Portal from Term 2).

1
Coach observesTaps 32 criteria green, yellow or red. Pencil or typed notes.

2
Save & LockSaved. The coach gets an edit link, the teacher a view link.
3
Feedback meetingThe coach updates the record. Close Lap pulses red until done.
4
Close LapThe record locks. Teacher and coach are emailed the 3 Next Steps.
5
Next lapThe last Next Steps show above the rubric.

COMINGTeacher reflects: “how did it go?”

COMINGTeacher plans: “my plan for the steps”
